Production & launch

  1. Own the destination. Reserve a short property route on the approved Harbison domain. Encode a permanent QR to that route, never directly to Vimeo or YouTube. Verify the URL and phone scan before printing; include a readable short URL as fallback.
  2. Cut a 60–90 second property film. Open on the best verified current view. Nathanael leads three specific visual beats, with real archive stills and process inserts. Match before and after framing where possible. Finish on a buyer invitation. Capture a fresh end-to-end walkthrough before paying to recover footage; reconsider recovery only if a critical documented upgrade is missing.
  3. Host playback, not the buyer relationship. I would start with Vimeo Free if the existing account permits a signed-out embed. Its current managed limits are 3 GB and 1 TB/month. YouTube embed is the $0 fallback; put the longer public build story on the channel. Cloudflare Stream starts at $5/month for storage plus playback usage if you later need more player control.
  4. Track interest simply. Use a dedicated QR campaign tag and carry it into the buyer form. Track scans, plays and inquiries. Verify it routes to the right owner with a labeled internal test before launch.
  5. Get facts and disclosures approved. No price, availability, concessions, permits or upgrades until verified. Confirm the listing/broker status before the QR goes out and place required licensed-name, license-number and responsible-broker details on applicable first-contact materials, including QR sign and page. Keep any rental/refinance fallback private.

Style references: TOLO, Park Drive, Los Angeles for clear before/after evidence; Ten Seventy, San Diego for warm residential imagery and restrained development presentation.
